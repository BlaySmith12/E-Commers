Troubleshooting

Payment Failures

Common Reasons
  • Insufficient funds in your account or Mobile Money wallet
  • Incorrect card number, PIN, or expiry date
  • Card or Mobile Money daily transaction limit reached
  • Network timeout due to poor internet connection
  • Bank or provider temporarily blocking the transaction
  • Session expired during checkout
What To Do
  • Double-check all your payment details before retrying
  • Ensure you have sufficient balance or credit
  • Try a different payment method (e.g., switch to Mobile Money)
  • Wait a few minutes and retry — the issue may be temporary
  • Clear your browser cache and start a new session
  • Contact your bank or Mobile Money provider to confirm no blocks
