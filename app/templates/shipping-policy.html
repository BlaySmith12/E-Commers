Delivery Timeframes

Other Regions

3 – 5 Business Days

Delivery to Ashanti, Western, Central, Eastern, and all other regional capitals.

Remote Areas

5 – 7 Business Days

Delivery to rural and hard-to-reach areas across all regions in Ghana.

Transparent Pricing

Shipping Fees

Standard Shipping

GHS 25 – GHS 50

Shipping fees vary depending on your delivery location and the size of your order. The exact fee will be calculated at checkout.

Free Shipping

Orders Over GHS 500

Enjoy free delivery on all orders totaling GHS 500 or more. No promo code needed — it's automatically applied at checkout.

Stay Informed

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an email with your tracking number. You can track your order at any time using:

  • Your order number on our Order Tracking page
  • The tracking link in your dispatch email
  • By contacting our support team directly
We've Got You Covered

Damaged Packages

We take great care in packaging your orders. However, if your package arrives damaged, please follow these steps:

  • Do not discard the packaging — keep all materials
  • Take clear photos of the damage
  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • We'll arrange a replacement or full refund
Please Note

Important Information

  • Delivery times may be affected during public holidays and festive seasons.
  • Ensure your delivery address and phone number are correct to avoid delays.
  • Our delivery partners will contact you before delivery to confirm availability.
  • For bulk or heavy item orders, additional delivery arrangements may apply.
  • Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed the next business day.
