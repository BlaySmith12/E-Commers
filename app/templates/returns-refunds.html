Hassle-Free Returns

Our Return Promise

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you're not happy with your order, we offer a straightforward 14-day return policy to make things right.

What You Need to Know

Return Policy Details

Eligible Products

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:

  • Returned within 14 days of delivery
  • Unused and in original condition
  • In original, undamaged packaging
  • With proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation)
  • All tags and labels still attached
Non-Returnable Products

The following items cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Custom-made or personalized orders
  • Clearance or final sale items
  • Opened hygiene products (toiletries, sanitizers)
  • Perishable goods
  • Items damaged by customer misuse
Easy Steps

How to Request a Return

1
Contact Us

Email support@asahsprimenest.com or call us to initiate your return request.

2
Get Authorization

Our team will review your request and provide you with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.

3
Pack & Ship

Pack the item securely in its original packaging and drop it off at the designated collection point or schedule a pickup.

4
Receive Refund

Once we receive and inspect your return, your refund will be processed within 5–10 business days.

Getting Your Money Back

Refunds & Exchanges

Refund Policy
  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after we receive the returned item
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout
  • Mobile Money refunds are sent directly to your registered number
  • Bank transfer refunds may take an additional 2–3 business days to appear
  • You will receive an email confirmation when your refund has been processed
Exchange Policy
  • Exchanges are available for items of equal or lesser value
  • If the replacement item costs more, you'll be charged the difference
  • If it costs less, the difference will be refunded to your account
  • Exchanges are subject to product availability
  • Contact us within 14 days of delivery to initiate an exchange
Quality Guarantee

Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, we will make it right. Follow these steps:

  • Document the damage with clear photos immediately
  • Contact us within 48 hours with your order number
  • We'll arrange a free return pickup or send a replacement
  • No return shipping cost for defective items
Changed Your Mind?

Order Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your order under the following conditions:

  • Before processing: Full refund, no questions asked
  • During processing: Contact us immediately; cancellation may still be possible
  • After dispatch: Order cannot be cancelled; use our return process instead
  • To cancel, email us or call during business hours

Need to Return an Item?

Our support team is here to help you with returns, refunds, and exchanges.
